# SU-47M Berkut Carrier Landing Checklist

**Left-side Panels - Forward Part:**

1. At 800ft, Set Tailhook DOWN

>

2. Turn left for the break (aka crosswind)

**Middle Panels - Bottom Left Side:**

3. Lower Landing Gear (White Knob), Slow to 8 AoA

>

4. Descend to 600ft
5. Contact Tower For Landing Information
6. Turn to Intercept Glideslope
7. Slow to AoA 8 (Adjust power accordingly)
8. At ¾ miles, LSO will call out “[CALLSIGN], Call the Ball”
   - Response format - “[CALLSIGN], Ball, [FUEL/1000]”
   - Example:
     - LSO - “Kilo 1-1, call the ball”
     - Pilot - “Kilo 1-1, Wasp Ball, 2.9”
   - NOTE: IF BALL NOT VISIBLE OR INSTRUCTIONS ARE REQUIRED EARLY, RESPOND “[CALLSIGN], Clara Ball” AND LSO WILL PROVIDE INSTRUCTIONS IMMEDIATELY RATHER THAN AT ¾ MILES

9. Follow the “Meatball” to landing: If the Light is low, so are you. If the Light is high, so are you. Try to keep the Ball in the Center (BALL WILL INCREASE BRIGHTNESS WHEN CENTRED)
10. In final stages of approach, follow LSO TO THE LETTER
11. Maintain AoA of 8. Do NOT Flare
12. Upon Contact, Advance Throttle to Afterburner
13. **(If Wire Caught)** Hold Afterburner for 4 seconds. Set Throttle IDLE. Set Hook UP, Set Wings UP, Taxi to Parking Area
14. **(If Wire Missed)** Bolter! (SEE WAVE OFF IN RELATED CHECKLISTS)
15. Monitor Fuel Levels
