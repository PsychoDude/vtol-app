# F-5E Tiger III Runway Takeoff Checklist

**BEFORE TAKEOFF – LINED UP**

1. Canopy – DOWN AND LOCKED
2. Nose Strut – EXTEND
3. RADAR – AS REQ
4. Flight Control – CHECK

**TAKEOFF**

1. Wheel Brakes – APPLY FIRM
2. Throttle – MIL
3. Engine Instruments – CHECK
4. Throttle – AB AS REQ
5. Wheel Brakes – Release
6. Nosewheel Steering – AS REQ

> **WARNING**

<div class="border-s-4 border-red-700 ps-4 my-5 flex flex-col gap-5">
<p>If nosewheel shimmy occurs, takeoff should be aborted if conditions permit.</p>
</div>

7. Aft Stick – 140 KIAS
8. 150 to 170 KIAS – ROTATE

> **NOTE**

<div class="border-s-4 border-blue-700 ps-4 my-5 flex flex-col gap-5">
<p>Takoff speed and full aft stick should be reached before aborting for non-rotation.</p>
<p>During takeoff with a heavyweight CL store, a noticeable hesitation may occur between nose strut extension and takeoff.</p>
</div>

**AFTER TAKEOFF**

1. Gear – UP
2. Flaps – AUTO/UP
3. Aux Intake Doors – CHECK CLOSE

> **NOTE**

<div class="border-s-4 border-blue-700 ps-4 my-5 flex flex-col gap-5">
<p>A high-pitched whine may occur as the nose gear starts up.</p>
</div>

**CLIMB**

1. External Fuel – CHECK IN EQUIPMENT
2. Stores JETT – CUED AS REQ
3. NAV Waypoints – ADJUSTED AS REQUIRED
