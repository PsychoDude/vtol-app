# F-5E Tiger III Runway Landing Checklist

**DESCENT AND PRE-LANDING CHECKS**

1. Armament Safety Check – COMPLETE
2. Radios – SET AS REQ
3. ILS – SET AS REQ
4. Exterior Lights – SET AS REQ
5. Altimeter Mode – SET AS REQ
6. Flaps – AUTO or MANEUVERING
7. Oxygen – CHECK
8. Shoulder Harness – SET AS REQ

**LANDING AND GO AROUND PATTERN**

1. Altimeter Mode – SET
2. Flaps – FULL
3. Gear – DOWN
4. Aux Door – CHECK OPEN
5. AoA – ON SPEED

> **WARNING**

<div class="border-s-4 border-red-700 ps-4 my-5 flex flex-col gap-5">
<p>Avoid wake turbulence. Allow a minimum of 2 minutes separation before landing behind a large multi-engine aircraft or helicopter. Time should be extended to 4 minutes behind extremely large aircraft. With a crosswind of more than 5 knots, the interval may be somewhat reduced, but attempt to remain above and upwind of the preceding aircraft’s flight path. Wake turbulence is most dangerous during the approach and flare prior to touchdown with calm or light crosswinds.</p>
</div>
