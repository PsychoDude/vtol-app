# F-5E Tiger III Startup Checklist

**ENGINE START CHECKLIST**

1. Battery – ON
2. Generator – ON – APU RPM GREEN
3. Seat – Adjust
4. Danger Areas Fore and Aft – CLEAR
5. Throttle – IDLE
6. Beacon – ON
7. Left Engine – ON – Cover
8. Right Engine – ON – Cover
9. Engine Instruments – Responsive
10. Generator – OFF

**BEFORE TAXI**

1. CMFDs – ON – Pages Configured
2. HUD – ON – De-Clutter as Req
3. RWR – ON
4. RADAR – OFF
5. NAV Lights – ON
6. Flight Controls – SET AND POSITIONED – FREE/CLEAR
7. Brakes – FIRM PRESS AND RELEASE
8. Landing/Taxi Lights – SET AS REQ
9. Flaps – SET AS REQ

**TAXI – AS CLEARED**

1. Wheel Brakes – SET FIRM
2. Brake Lock – OFF
3. Flight Instruments – CHECK
4. Navigation Waypoints – SET
5. Radios – SET
6. EQUIP Page – CONFIG SET

> **CAUTION:**

<div class="border-s-4 border-red-700 ps-4 my-5 flex flex-col gap-5">
<p>Nominal refers to the RPM being in the green zone of the dial. Starting engines before the APU is fully spun up will lead to ignition failure. </p>

<p>In the event of ignition failure, shut down both engines, and confirm APU RPM NOMINAL. Once NOMINAL, repeat steps 5 and 6.</p>
</div>
